About Me

A student who builds real things

Design-minded, detail-obsessed, and always learning.

I'm Allen V. De Lemos, an aspiring college student and front-end developer who loves turning ideas into clean, functional websites. What started as curiosity about how the web works has grown into a passion for building interfaces that people genuinely enjoy using.

On the front end, I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and Tailwind CSS, designing in Figma and managing my code with Git & GitHub. On the back end, I build with PHP and MySQL — so I can take a project from a static design all the way to a working, data-driven application.

Alongside my projects, I also do laboratory activities and hands-on school work — building small programs, practicing databases, and completing tech exercises — which keeps my fundamentals sharp every semester.

I'm currently open to freelance and entry-level opportunities. If you need someone reliable who cares about the details, let's talk.
